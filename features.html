Feature Details
🔐 Privacy

No activity logs, by design

We never record which sites you visit, what you transmit, or your real IP address. We keep no connection history in logs — on our side or yours. If you use the browser as your client, we recommend private tabs and similar tools so nothing is stored on your device.
🌐 Reliability

Works on difficult networks

NullVPN continuously measures the quality of every available path between you and our infrastructure and keeps the best working route active — automatically.
💎 Payments

Pay your way

Debit/credit cards or crypto — processed securely by certified third-party payment providers. We never see or store your full card details; we receive only a confirmation.
⚡ Speed

Fast enough for video calls

Premium European servers optimized for low latency across the EU, MENA, and South Asia. Streaming, video calls, and browsing at full speed.
📱 Accessibility

Multiple ways to connect

Android app for phones and tablets, plus a router application that protects every device on your home network — smart TVs, consoles, and IoT included.

Designed around one goal

Every feature serves one purpose: a connection that reliably works wherever you are — mobile data, fixed broadband, or demanding networks. Privacy and reliability are not add-ons here; they are the product.

If one access channel is unavailable, another one works. If the website is unreachable, Telegram and the Web3 mirror still deliver your configuration.
