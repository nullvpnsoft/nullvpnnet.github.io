Not just another shared-server VPN

Many consumer VPNs route thousands of strangers through the same shared servers and well-known address ranges — which makes connections crowded, slow, and unreliable when you need them most.

NullVPN gives you a private tunnel with dedicated endpoints and automatic backup paths. Your traffic stays encrypted from your device all the way out, and if one route has trouble, another takes over. Reliability by architecture, not luck.
